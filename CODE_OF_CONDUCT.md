# Code of Conduct

**English** · [简体中文](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md)

AI4Math Chronicle is an evidence-focused research and open-source community. Participation should make the historical record more accurate, reviewable, and useful.

## Expected conduct

- Discuss claims, evidence, methodology, and code rather than attacking people.
- Disagree precisely and provide sources when challenging factual or editorial decisions.
- Be respectful toward contributors with different levels of mathematical, engineering, or language expertise.
- Keep reviews focused on the contribution and explain requested changes clearly.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information without permission.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, spam, sustained personal attacks, or knowingly disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

Good-faith disagreement over H1/H2/H3 classification, verification, mathematical claims, or project direction is welcome when it remains evidence-based and civil.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies to repository Issues, Pull Requests, reviews, project discussions, and other spaces used to represent or coordinate AI4Math Chronicle.

Maintainers may edit, hide, remove, lock, or report content that violates this policy and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the project and its contributors. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate and documented when practical.

For security-sensitive matters, follow the [Security Policy](./SECURITY.md).
